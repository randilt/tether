Tether

Set up once, then share your camera or mic with your PC.

One-time setup: trust this PC

Your phone needs to trust a small security file from your PC. Without this, the camera button will not work.

  1. Tap the button below to download the certificate file.
  2. Open the Settings app (grey gears).
  3. Near the top, tap Profile Downloaded. (If you don’t see it: General → VPN & Device Management.)
  4. Tap Install, enter your phone passcode if asked, then Install again. Tap Done.
  5. Still in Settings: General → About → Certificate Trust Settings.
  6. Turn on Full Trust for Tether Local CA.
  7. Come back to this Safari tab and tap Continue.
